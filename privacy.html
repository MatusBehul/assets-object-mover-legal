Privacy policy

Assets Object Mover for Jira sends nothing outside Atlassian and keeps only a log of each run on your own site.

Assets Object Mover for Jira ("the app") is provided by William Wadby, s. r. o., Tupého 34, 831 01 Bratislava, Slovakia, company ID (IČO) 53583671 ("we"). This policy explains what the app does with data. Last updated 26 October 2026.

What the app accesses

While you use it, the app reads Assets schemas, object types and objects, creates and updates objects, deletes the originals it moved, and updates the Assets fields of Jira issues linked to them. It does this as you, with your own permissions, and only while you have the app open.

What the app stores

So a run can pause and carry on, the app saves its settings and progress, the ids of the objects it works on, and a log of each object's old and new key and name. Object names can contain personal data if your Assets holds it, for example people's names. All of this is stored in Atlassian's Forge storage for your site. It is deleted when you click Clear and start another, or when you uninstall the app.

What leaves Atlassian

Nothing. The app runs entirely on Atlassian's Forge platform and has no servers of its own. The log you download is created in your browser.

What we can see

We have no access to your Assets data, your Jira issues or your users' data. Atlassian gives us licensing and installation details for the site, such as the site address and subscription status, which we use only to provide and bill for the app. When you contact support, we keep what you send us for as long as we need it to answer you.

Your rights

For the run log stored on your site, your organization is the controller and can delete it at any time as described above. Under the GDPR you can ask us about any data we hold about you at any time, and you can complain to the Slovak data protection authority (Úrad na ochranu osobných údajov Slovenskej republiky).
